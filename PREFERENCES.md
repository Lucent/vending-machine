## What the ranking says

- **Dense over aerated.** Nougat, malt, and chalk all went in the bin. Caramel,
  peanut butter, toffee, and wafer all stayed. This one rule explains most of the bin.
- **Chocolate is the wrapper, not the point.** No plain chocolate has been ranked.
  Hershey's went in the bin while Reese's and Caramello tied at #1. Milk vs dark
  does not register.
- **Peanut butter yes, whole peanuts no.** PayDay, Peanut M&M's, Snickers, Baby Ruth,
  Goo Goo Cluster, Salted Nut Roll, and Take 5 binned. Reese's, PB M&M's, Reese's
  Pieces, Butterfinger, and Reese's Sticks ranked. Almonds are tolerated.
- **Caramel must be soft and attached to something.** Chewy caramel with chocolate
  or cream ranks (Caramello, Milk Duds, Riesen, Rolo, Caramel Creams, Cow Tales).
  Hard caramel (Werther's) and jaw-workout caramel (Sugar Daddy) do not. But
  "more caramel" is not a safe bet: Caramel M&M's, Ghirardelli Caramel squares,
  and Milky Way Simply Caramel all got binned.
- **The peppermint thing is really white chocolate + salt + crunch.** Peppermint
  Pretzel Crisps sit at #9. Pure soft peppermint got binned twice (Bob's Sweet
  Stripes, Brach's Peppermint Nougats). King Leo is ranked last and kept for the tin.
- **Brand loyalty is zero.** Mars lost Snickers, Milky Way, and M&M's but kept
  Twix. Hershey lost its own bar but kept everything labeled Reese's.
- **Ranks are categories.** Eight tiers are ties, mostly of near-identical products
  (Skor/Heath, Caramel Creams/Cow Tales). A tie means "same thing to me."
- **Fruit survives only as a dense chew.** Skittles and Mike and Ike stayed. Sour,
  gummy, hard, and powder all left.

## Caveat

The bin means one of three things: don't like it, never tried it ("lots of
these I'm not familiar with"), or too similar to something already ranked and
it did not earn a spot (user's words; all Corn Nuts are pretty similar, for
example). Treat a binned suggestion as weak evidence until confirmed. A binned
variant of a ranked item usually means redundant, not disliked.

- King Leo Soft Peppermint Sticks, the powdery ones in the red tin. Made by
  Standard Candy, Nashville. Hard to find locally; stock is spotty online.

## What the ranking says

- **Cheese dust on a corn carrier is the whole top nine.** Cheez Balls, Harvest
  Cheddar, Nacho Doritos, cheddar pretzel pieces, both Cheetos, Ritz Toasted
  Cheddar, Combos. Cheese baked into a cracker ranks last (Cheez-It, Cheese Nips).
- **Potato is the worst carrier.** Every plain potato chip binned, kettle ones
  included. Potato survives only under BBQ (Lay's BBQ, Zapp's Voodoo).
- **Sweet-savory beats heat.** Honey BBQ is #1, BBQ and Voodoo rank, every hot
  item is binned. Grippo's (spicy BBQ) binned while Lay's BBQ ranked.
- **Sour cream, onion, and ranch are the nougat of this shelf.** Cool Ranch,
  Lay's SC&O, Pringles SC&O, Ruffles Cheddar & Sour Cream all binned.
- **Plain anything is dead.** No unseasoned chip, pretzel, cracker, or popcorn
  ranked. Only nuts rank plain.
- **Nuts have one right form each.** Soft nuts raw (pecan, walnut), hard nuts
  roasted (cashew, pistachio). Almonds have no good form. Peanuts only boiled.
- **Nostalgia alone does not rank.** The discontinued batch went in the bin
  except Cheez Balls (#1). Same caveat as candy: bin mixes "never tried",
  "don't like", and "too similar to something already ranked."

## Cross-shelf

Both shelves reward a strong coating on a dense crunchy base (toffee shatter,
peanut butter, cheese dust, honey BBQ) and punish bland carriers (plain
chocolate, plain potato), aerated texture (nougat, popcorn), and anything
whose main note is sour or hot.
